# Lilongwe Adventist Hospital Website Proposal

## Email draft

**Subject: Proposal for the Lilongwe Adventist Hospital Website**

Dear [Owner's Name],

I am pleased to share a proposal for the Lilongwe Adventist Hospital website. The website is designed to give patients and visitors a clear, welcoming way to learn about the hospital, explore available care, prepare for a visit and contact the team.

The site combines practical patient information with a modern presentation of the hospital’s services and history. Its main sections are outlined below.

### Pages

- **Home** — Introduces the hospital with rotating care imagery, a clear appointment call to action, quick links to important patient resources, a short introduction to the hospital, featured services and information about why patients may choose the hospital.
- **About Us** — Presents the hospital’s history, mission and vision, care principles, leadership information and key facts such as its location, opening hours and bed capacity.
- **Services** — Provides an overview of the hospital’s departments and core services. Individual service pages give visitors more detail about each service, what care it covers, clinic schedules and how to get in touch.
- **Doctors** — Introduces the medical team and provides individual profiles with specialty and professional information.
- **Patient Information** — Helps patients prepare for a visit, with guidance on appointments, what to bring, payment and insurance enquiries, emergency care, visiting hours and frequently asked questions.
- **Book an Appointment** — Allows a patient to send an appointment request and provides the hospital’s contact details, location, emergency guidance and specialist clinic schedule.
- **Health & Wellbeing Blog** — Shares general health and wellbeing articles that visitors can browse by topic.

### Visitor features

- Responsive layouts for phones, tablets and desktop screens.
- Rotating homepage photographs with no manual slide controls.
- Direct links to call the hospital, view directions and contact reception.
- Online appointment request form with a reference number when the request is received; reception follows up to confirm a time.
- Service and doctor profile pages to help visitors find relevant information.
- Patient guidance and clinic schedule information to help visitors prepare.
- Health article topic filters.
- A restricted staff area for reviewing appointment requests and updating their status.

### Main content presented

The website introduces Lilongwe Adventist Hospital as a private healthcare facility on Presidential Way in Lilongwe, Malawi. It presents the hospital as operating 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and notes its history dating to 1983.

The services covered include ophthalmology, dental care, obstetrics and gynaecology, surgical services, internal medicine, orthopedics, pediatrics and core hospital services. Core services listed include inpatient and outpatient care, an operating theatre, laboratory, pharmacy, radiology/X-ray, ultrasound, ambulance services, physiotherapy and ART clinics.

Insurance information directs patients to contact the hospital in advance to confirm scheme acceptance and any pre-authorisation requirements. Appointment requests are presented as requests, with reception responsible for confirming the appointment details.
